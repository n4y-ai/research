### Конфликты между источниками

1. **E35 vs E36:** Square-root rewards (E35) dampen large-stakeholder dominance, while quadratic voting (E36) amplifies preference intensity. These serve different purposes: E35 addresses reward distribution, E36 addresses governance signaling. Not a true conflict but requires careful layer separation in N4Y.AI design.
2. **E48 vs E49:** Seesaw principle (E48) recommends demand-side subsidies, while E49 finds innovation strategies outperform subsidies. Resolution: subsidies for bootstrap phase only, transition to innovation-driven retention.
3. **G1 gap persists:** No source provides a completed Nash equilibrium proof for the specific 40/20/30/10% split. E28 (Shelby) provides methodology, E29 provides theoretical bounds, E30 provides validator-level proof. A custom formal analysis applying Shelby methodology to N4Y.AI's revenue split remains required.

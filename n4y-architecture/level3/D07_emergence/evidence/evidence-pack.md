## Source Conflicts & Flags

1. **Containment effectiveness conflict:** E6 (Haldane & May) suggests compartmentalization reduces systemic risk substantially, but E7 (Battiston et al.) warns that complex interconnections can bypass compartment boundaries. **Resolution needed:** Microsandbox must be tested for cross-sandbox contagion via CSB (Cross-Sandbox Bridge).

2. **Heterogeneity trade-off:** E3 (Epstein/Axtell) and E48 (Axelrod) agree that heterogeneous populations are more stable, but MCP deep_research notes coordination complexity increases 2-3× with diversity. **Trade-off:** Stability vs. coordination overhead — needs quantification for N4Y.AI agent density levels.

3. **Stigmergy density threshold discrepancy:** E16 reports ρ_c ≈ 0.23, while MCP deep_research synthesis suggests ρ_c ≈ 0.25-0.35. **Likely resolution:** Different experimental conditions; E16 is empirical with specific agent architecture, broader range from theoretical models.

4. **Collusion detection vs. cooperation:** E37 (GNN detection) and E38 (algorithmic collusion) highlight that tacit collusion by LLM agents can be nearly indistinguishable from legitimate cooperation without communication monitoring. **Flag:** N4Y.AI needs explicit criteria distinguishing Alliance (legitimate) from Cartel (harmful) beyond behavioral signals alone.

5. **LLM-specific dynamics gap:** MCP deep_research explicitly flags that application of traditional MAS theory to LLM-based economies is largely unexplored. Specific numerical thresholds (density, trust) are extrapolated from other domains and need empirical validation on actual LLM agent systems.

6. **Collective LOA theoretical gap:** No direct research on whether a group of AI agents can exceed any individual member's autonomy level. E46 and E47 address delegation and decomposition but not autonomy amplification per se. **Flag:** G-07 remains the weakest-covered critical concept.

## Remaining Holes

- **G-08 (Network effects validation):** No empirical studies validating Metcalfe/Reed scaling in AI agent economies specifically. Financial network studies exist but don't directly validate value-scaling laws for agent networks. Needs targeted ABM simulation.
- **G-07 (Collective LOA):** Conceptual support exists (delegation decomposition, collective intelligence) but no formal framework for "collective autonomy level" that exceeds individual LOA bounds. This is a novel N4Y.AI concept requiring original theoretical work.
- **Empirical validation gap:** Most evidence on flash crash containment, stigmergy thresholds, and organizational form conditions is from simulation or non-AI domains. Real LLM agent economy data is scarce (2024-2026 literature is nascent).
